How I Work
AI-POWERED
CREATIVE.
Faster. Smarter. Still 100% Original.

I don't just use AI as a gimmick — I've built it into my actual edit workflow. Whether it's generating cinematic B-roll for a reel, building motion poster elements, or producing voiceovers and concept frames before a shoot, these tools let me move at a pace a one-person production team simply couldn't otherwise. Every tool below is something I actively use on real client work — not just played around with.

Copywriting · Strategy
Claude
Anthropic's Claude helps me write ad copy, caption strategies, brand positioning docs, and campaign briefs — fast and on-brand.
Copy · Ideas · Research
ChatGPT
Used for quick ideation, hashtag research, competitor analysis and drafting initial content frameworks for social media strategies.
Text · Multimodal
Gemini
Google Gemini for real-time research, image understanding and generating multilingual captions — especially useful for local Mumbai market content.
AI Filmmaking
Google Flow
Google's AI filmmaking studio, built on Veo. I use it to build a sequence shot by shot — keeping the camera, look and characters consistent across a whole scene instead of one-off clips.
High-Quality Video
Kling AI
My go-to for high-quality AI video — clean motion, believable physics and detail that holds up at full resolution. Used for B-roll and product shots that need to cut straight into a reel.
Generative Fill · Images
Firefly
Adobe Firefly inside Photoshop for generative fill, background extension and removing objects from product shots — saves hours of retouching.
Typography · Posters
Ideogram
The best AI for generating images with accurate text — I use it for social media posters, ad copy layouts and typographic visual concepts.
Video · Motion
Runway ML
Used for AI video generation, motion effects and turning static product images into smooth video clips for brand reels.
